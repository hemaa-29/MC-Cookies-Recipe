Minecraft Cookies Recipe

Learn how to make minecraft cookies in real life!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of shortening
  • 3/4 cup white sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp. vanilla extract
  • 3/4 tsp. Baking powder (Has to be baking powder)
  • 1/4 tsp. Baking soda (use these exact measurements)
  • 2 3/4 - 3 cups flour
  • A handful of chocolate chips
  • 1 pair of hands (or feet)

    • Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 180 Celcius
    2. Combine shortening and sugar, then mix eggs in.
    3. Mix baking powder and flour separately and then mix it in with the first mixture.
    4. Now you have the dough ready, just flatten and make cookie shapes!
